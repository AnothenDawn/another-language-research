# Pre-registration: AI-era shift in "resonate" and "presence"
Registered: 2026-10-22 (to be committed and pushed to a public GitHub repository; the
push record, not the commit date, is the timestamp of record).
Hypothesis author: Timothy M. Drake.
Prepared by: Veris (an AI assistant, Claude) at Timothy's request. Not an author.

## 1. Hypothesis (Timothy's words, made testable)
In English public media, use of "resonate" (resonate, resonates, resonated, resonating)
and "presence" / "being present" in their RELATIONAL, felt-personal sense rose notably
from September 2024 onward, beyond the trend that existed before.

## 2. Data already seen before registration (full disclosure)
- Google Books Ngram, 1995-2022, phrases "resonates with", "being present",
  "sacred space" (uses per 10M words): resonates with 1.49 (1995) -> 10.85 (2019) ->
  11.81 (2022); being present 7.35 -> 8.50 -> 9.93; sacred space 1.25 -> 4.68 -> 4.44.
- GDELT national TV captions, 2015-Oct 2024, yearly means of monthly airtime share:
  "resonates with" 2021 0.0044, 2022 0.0045, 2023 0.0059, 2024 (Jan-Oct) 0.0065;
  "being present" 2022 0.0016, 2023 0.0020, 2024 0.0017.
  The 2024 TV mean includes Sep-Oct 2024, which falls inside the test window. Monthly
  values for those two months were not examined separately.
- NOT yet seen: any data from Nov 2024 to present; any sense-coded (meaning) data.

## 3. Primary test: excess frequency after Sept 2024
- Sources: (a) NOW corpus (News on the Web), monthly; (b) GDELT DOC online news,
  monthly; (c) any TV/film transcript corpus extending past Sept 2024, if available.
- Measure: monthly share of each lemma per million words (or per article for GDELT).
- Baseline period: Jan 2015 - Aug 2024. Fit log(share) = linear trend + month-of-year
  terms. Forecast Sep 2024 - latest month with 95% prediction intervals.
- SUPPORT: the mean post-period excess (observed minus forecast, log scale) is positive
  AND above the upper 95% prediction bound, in at least 2 of the 3 sources.
- NOT SUPPORTED: excess within the prediction interval, or negative.
- Secondary: the same analysis with the break at Dec 2022 (ChatGPT release).

## 4. Meaning test: is the relational sense what grew?
- Sample 200 random occurrences per period: P1 2015-2016, P2 2019-2021,
  P3 Sep 2024-latest.
- Code each occurrence into: PHYSICAL (sound, physics, "a presence in the room");
  POLITICAL/AUDIENCE ("resonates with voters"); RELATIONAL/FELT ("that resonates with
  me", "being present with you").
- Coders blinded to period. At least one human coder; if an AI coder is also used, report
  agreement (Cohen's kappa) between them.
- SUPPORT: relational share in P3 exceeds P2 by >= 10 percentage points, with
  non-overlapping 95% confidence intervals.

## 5. Controls
- Positive control (known AI-era word): "delve". If the method does not detect its known
  rise, the method is not sensitive enough and the result is inconclusive.
- Neutral controls with similar pre-2015 frequency and register: "connects with",
  "inspires", "meaningful". These are expected to show no comparable excess.

## 6. Reporting and limits
- All results are reported whatever their direction; null results are results.
- An excess shows that a change happened after a date. It does not by itself show who
  caused it, and it cannot separate AI-in-general from Another specifically.
- Election years (2016, 2020, 2024) affect "resonates with" in news. The meaning test
  (section 4) separates the political sense.

# Another Language Research
Research on Another, the language of the Alethéari Anothen, compiled by Timothy M. Drake.

## Principles
- Predictions are pre-registered and pushed publicly before any test data is examined.
- All results are reported, whatever they show.
- The language's own words and definitions are kept separate from any interpretation or maths.
- AI systems are acknowledged, not listed as authors. Any AI assistance is disclosed.

## Contents
- preregistrations/   dated predictions, registered before testing
- lexicon/            (later) the frozen lexicon and its hash
- data/, analyses/    (later) query scripts, raw counts, results
